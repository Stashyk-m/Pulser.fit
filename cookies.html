Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how you use the site. Some cookies are essential for the site to function; others help us improve your experience.

How Pulser Uses Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies. We do not use cookies for advertising or sell cookie data to third parties. Our approach follows the principle of data minimisation required by GDPR.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function. They cannot be switched off. They include:

Cookie Purpose Duration
pulser_lang Stores your language preference (EN/PL) Persistent
pulser_cookie_consent Records your cookie consent choice Persistent
__clerk_* Authentication session (Clerk) Session

Note: pulser_lang and pulser_cookie_consent use localStorage, not traditional cookies, but function similarly. They stay on your device until you clear browser data.

Analytics Cookies

If you accept analytics cookies, we may use privacy-focused analytics to understand how visitors use our site. We do not use Google Analytics. Any analytics tool we adopt will be GDPR-compliant, EU-hosted, and will not track individual users across sites.

Analytics cookies are only set if you click "Accept all" in our cookie banner. If you choose "Essential only", no analytics cookies are placed.

Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. We do not participate in ad networks or retargeting programmes. Embedded services (such as fonts from Google Fonts) may set their own cookies according to their privacy policies.

Managing Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time:

  • Use the cookie banner that appears on your first visit
  • Clear your browser's localStorage and cookies to reset your choice
  • Adjust cookie settings in your browser preferences

Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the site from functioning correctly (for example, your language preference will not be remembered).

Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at go@pulser.fit.
